## SEO and AI search: what changes

Traditional search engines show a list of links; AI assistants generate an answer and cite a handful of sources. To be one of those sources, your site needs what good SEO has always needed, done with more rigour:

- **Crawlable, indexable content**, served as HTML rather than hidden behind JavaScript.
- **Clear, direct answers** to the questions your customers ask.
- **Structured data** describing your company, services and FAQs.
- **A consistent brand entity**: the same name, description and details on your site and external profiles.
- **Authority and mentions** on reputable sites in your industry.

## What an SEO audit covers

| Area | What we review |
|---|---|
| Crawling & indexing | robots.txt, sitemap, canonicals, redirects, 404 errors |
| Performance | Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS), page weight, images |
| Content | Search intent, duplication, cannibalization, thin content |
| Structure | Architecture, internal linking, click depth |
| Structured data | Validity and consistency of schema.org markup |
| International | hreflang tags and language versions |
| AI visibility | How your brand appears in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews |

## SEO without shortcuts

We only use techniques that follow Google's guidelines. No bulk link buying and no low-quality automated content: those tactics can produce a temporary spike but end up getting a site penalised.
