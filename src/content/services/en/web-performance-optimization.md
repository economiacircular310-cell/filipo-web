## Why speed matters

Every second of waiting increases the chance a visitor leaves. A fast website improves three things at once: **user experience**, **Google rankings** and **conversion rate**.

## Core Web Vitals thresholds

| Metric | What it measures | Good |
|---|---|---|
| LCP | Time until the main content is displayed | ≤ 2.5 s |
| INP | How quickly the page responds to interactions | ≤ 200 ms |
| CLS | Unexpected layout shifts while loading | ≤ 0.1 |

Google assesses these metrics at the 75th percentile of real visits to your site, on both mobile and desktop.

## Common optimizations

- Serving images in modern formats (AVIF, WebP) at the right size.
- Loading fonts efficiently and avoiding text shifts.
- Removing unnecessary JavaScript and deferring non-critical code.
- Generating pages statically or on the server whenever possible.
- Using a CDN and correct caching headers.
- Auditing third-party scripts (analytics, chat widgets, ads), often the main cause of slowness.
